## Required prerequisites

Complete `01.node_fundamentals` before starting Session 1. It gives students the Node.js, HTTP, npm, asynchronous-code, configuration, and debugging knowledge that Express requires.

## Optional enrichment

Teach these after the academic core, or offer them to students who want deeper backend skills:

- CORS protocol internals: `02.http_and_express/06.cors_under_the_hood.md`
- Advanced queries, pagination, sorting, and search: `04.advanced_api_engineering/02` through `05`
- Production API features: all of `05.production_api_features`
- Backend architecture, distributed systems, Docker, and Kubernetes: all of `07.backend_architecture`
- Additional practice assignments: `08.assignments/01` through `04`, `06`, and `07`
